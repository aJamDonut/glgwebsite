Studio profile

Built like a playable lab.

We are a small team that prototypes quickly, hunts for the fun, and commits only to mechanics with meaningful impact.

History & Future

Our founders met in 2008 and began collaborating on online 2D and text based games. With over ten free games already being released with over 1 million plays, they decided to create GreenLabGames in 2024 to begin launching commercial games into the world.

Community Growth

Our goal is to create games that foster community and creativity in player storytelling. We want to hear player stories, dramas, epic events and every bizarre little interaction our players enjoy.
